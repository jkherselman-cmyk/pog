Our Story

The Presence of Greatness

POG African Safaris was founded on a simple belief: that the greatest hunting experiences are about more than the harvest. They are about the people you meet, the friendships you build, and the memories you create along the way.

Nestled in the spectacular Eastern Cape of South Africa, we offer authentic fair-chase hunting experiences in some of the country's most diverse and beautiful landscapes. From sweeping valleys and rugged mountains to expansive plains rich with wildlife, our hunting areas provide both challenge and unforgettable scenery.

The name POG was born from a joke between friends about being in the "Presence of Greatness." Yet during a safari together, they discovered that the true greatness was not found in themselves, but in the experience surrounding them—the beauty of Africa, the abundance of wildlife, the warmth of genuine hospitality, and the people who dedicate themselves to making every hunt exceptional.

Today, that spirit remains the foundation of POG African Safaris. We strive to ensure that every guest leaves with more than trophies; they leave with stories, friendships, and memories that will last a lifetime.

The Team

Meet Your Hosts

Willie van Zyl & Family

Founder, Outfitter & PH

Willie van Zyl is the founder, Outfitter, and Professional Hunter of POG African Safaris. Holding Professional Hunter permits in the Eastern Cape, Western Cape, and Free State, Willie brings years of experience, knowledge, and passion to every hunt.

Known for his sense of humor, dedication to his clients, and deep respect for wildlife, Willie strives to make every safari a truly memorable experience. His philosophy is simple: every hunter deserves a safari that exceeds expectations and creates memories that last a lifetime.

Alongside Willie is his wife, Junita, and their children, Leras and Ineke. As a family, they share a love for animals, hunting, shooting sports, and the African outdoors. Their warmth and hospitality help create the welcoming atmosphere that guests have come to know and appreciate at POG African Safaris.

Marius Heinen & Family

Professional Hunter & Lodge Management

Marius Heinen is a Professional Hunter licensed in the Eastern Cape and a key member of the POG African Safaris team. He is the owner of a fifth-generation working farm and hunting area, where tradition, conservation, and responsible land management have been passed down through generations.

Together with his wife, Ansu, Marius manages the lodge and helps ensure that every guest enjoys a comfortable and authentic African safari experience. Their daughter, Milan, is being raised with the same appreciation for nature, wildlife, and family values that define both families.

Marius and Ansu take great pride in sharing their home, their land, and their way of life with visitors from around the world.
