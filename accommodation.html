The Lodge

Comfort meets nature

After a rewarding day in the African bush, there is nothing better than returning to a warm lodge, good company, and a hearty meal.

Our lodge offers comfortable accommodations designed to help guests relax and enjoy every moment of their safari experience. Managed by Marius and Ansu Heinen, guests can expect genuine hospitality, personal attention, and a welcoming atmosphere throughout their stay.

Evenings are often spent around the campfire sharing stories from the day's hunt, enjoying wonderful meals, and experiencing the true spirit of African safari life.

Whether you are traveling alone, with friends, or with family, our goal is to make you feel completely at home from the moment you arrive.

Because at POG African Safaris, you are not treated as a customer—you are welcomed as family.
