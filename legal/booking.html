Booking Agreement

Deposits

To confirm and secure your safari dates, a deposit equivalent to the current daily rates for the full duration of the scheduled hunt is required at the time of booking.

Once the booking deposit has been received, optional prepayment arrangements for the remaining balance of your quoted safari may be made prior to the commencement of the hunt upon request, although such prepayment is not required.

Balance

Upon completion of the safari, the final account will be reconciled. Any trophy fees for animals included in the original quotation but not harvested will be deducted from the final invoice. Additional charges for animals harvested, wounded animals, extended hunting days, and any other expenses not included in the original quotation will be added to the final account.

Please note that all wounded animals will be charged at the full applicable trophy fee, regardless of whether they are recovered.

The remaining balance of the safari, including any additional charges incurred during the hunt, must be paid in full prior to departure.

All final accounts are to be settled before leaving the hunting property.
