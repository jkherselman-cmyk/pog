Firearms Import Guide

Bringing Your Rifle to South Africa

South Africa welcomes international hunters bringing their own firearms for hunting purposes.

Required Documents

Prior to travel, hunters should have:

  • Valid Passport
  • Return Airline Ticket
  • Firearm License or Firearm Registration Certificate from Country of Residence
  • Completed Temporary Firearm Import Permit Application (SAPS 520)
  • Invitation Letter from POG African Safaris
  • Safari Booking Confirmation

Recommended Calibers

While caliber choice is personal, commonly used calibers for plains game include:

  • .243 Winchester
  • .270 Winchester
  • 7mm Remington Magnum
  • .308 Winchester
  • .30-06 Springfield
  • .300 Winchester Magnum

Ammunition Regulations

  • Transport ammunition separately as required by airline regulations.
  • Maximum ammunition allowance is subject to airline and South African regulations.
  • Ammunition should be packed in original factory containers whenever possible.

Firearm Storage

Firearms must remain under the control of the owner or stored securely as directed by your Professional Hunter.

Firearm Rental

POG African Safaris offers quality rental rifles for hunters who prefer not to travel internationally with firearms.

Details regarding availability, calibers, and rental rates can be provided upon request.

Arrival Assistance

POG African Safaris will provide:

  • Invitation Letter
  • Booking Confirmation
  • Hunting Permit Information
  • Assistance with firearm import procedures where required
